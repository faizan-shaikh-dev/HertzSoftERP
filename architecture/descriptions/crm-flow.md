# Hertzsoft Business Operations Flow

## Dual-Stream Operations Architecture

```text
                  ┌──► Student Lead ──► Course Enrollment ────► Course / Internship Certificate
Inquiry / Lead ───┤
                  └──► Client Lead  ──► Project Kickoff   ────► Payments Tracking & Deliverables
```

### 1. Student Stream (Education & Training)
- **Courses**:
  - Data Science
  - FullStack Development
  - Digital Marketing
- **Outcome**:
  - Enrolled as Student / Intern
  - Issued Offer Letter
  - Issued Internship / Course Completion Certificate upon completion

### 2. Client Stream (Software Projects)
- **Projects**:
  - Custom Web & Software Development projects
  - Tracked via deadline, status, and milestone delivery
- **Financials**:
  - Total project contract value
  - Installment payments tracking (UPI, Bank, etc.)
  - Real-time pending balance calculation
